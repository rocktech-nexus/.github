## RockTech NeXus

Engineering software for preliminary rock mechanics and surface blasting.

Every result shows its method, units, assumptions and warnings. Any value we
could not trace to a published source is labelled engineering judgement or
withdrawn, and our validation register is open.

### What is here

- **[rtnx-methods](https://github.com/rocktech-nexus/rtnx-methods)** —
  open implementations of published rock mechanics and blasting methods, with
  test vectors taken from worked examples in the literature.
- **[validation-register](https://github.com/rocktech-nexus/validation-register)** —
  what we checked, against which source, and what we could not verify.

### Products

**Rocklytics** covers geomechanics: rock mass classification, rock strength,
tunnel stress and support, slope stability.
**mineraX** covers surface blasting: blast design, fragmentation, ground
vibration screening.

Both are in closed beta. Access by request at
[rocktechnexus.com/beta](https://rocktechnexus.com/beta).

### Scope

These are preliminary assessment and screening tools. They do not replace site
investigation, numerical modelling, detailed design or statutory compliance.
The qualified engineer keeps the final judgement.
